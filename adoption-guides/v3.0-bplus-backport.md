# Adoption guide — v3.0 "Option B+" backport directive

**For adopters who vendored KB-Orchestrator-Core at or before commit `b1dea47` (INV 1.A residue) and have NOT yet pulled `180696a` / `381c939` / `18f6b54`.** Closes the gap introduced by the v3.0 Option B+ sprint — INVARIANT 11 (minimum-viable context, principle-centric), descriptive bundles, the dispatch-shim ledger schema bump, and the Tier-1 audit fixes that affect adopter-facing surfaces (H-1, H-3, M-1, M-5, M-6, L-1, L-2).

If you adopted at or after commit `18f6b54` ("INV 11 — sync Step 10 consume row JSON example with schema") your tree already contains everything in this guide; you can disregard.

---

## Why this exists

Between `b1dea47` and `18f6b54` the framework promoted two principle-centric changes that adopters cannot derive by re-reading their vendored snapshot:

1. **INVARIANT 11 — minimum-viable context per role.** Every dispatched role MUST load minimum-viable context and MUST NOT load `SYSTEM-BLUEPRINT.md` for runtime work. The principle binds the *outcome* (no monolith, role-scoped context, recorded in the ledger), not the *mechanism*. Bundles remain the framework's recommended mechanism, but adopters MAY substitute semantic routing, dynamic composition, or RAG-style retrieval as long as the outcome holds.
2. **`agents.config.yaml` schema_version 2 → 3.** Adds the REQUIRED `family:` field on every agent and the contract-surface implications of INV 1.A's two-timing validation reconciliation.

Plus seven Tier-1 audit fixes whose old wording misled adopters in production-relevant ways (worker write authority, codex-implement binding, codex-bridge degradation, soak-day-5 step semantics, two stale references in the external-orchestrator directive).

This guide is normative for projects pinning a pre-`180696a` snapshot; pull it in *before* the next dispatch-shim run that you intend to audit.

## Section 1 — The Backport Directive (drop-in)

Paste this paragraph into your orchestrator's persistent system prompt, alongside the existing KB-Orchestrator-Core directive. It supersedes any earlier "10 invariants" / "load the bundle" / "workers write all artifacts" wording.

```text
COPY START — KB-Orchestrator-Core v3.0 Option B+ backport directive

Effective immediately: the framework now declares 11 invariants, not 10. The new INVARIANT 11 — minimum-viable context per role — requires that every dispatched role load only the context that role needs, never SYSTEM-BLUEPRINT.md for runtime work, and that the resolved load be recorded in the DISPATCH ledger row's context_sources field with the selection mechanism named in context_selection_mechanism. Bundles (bundles/<role>.yaml) are the framework's recommended mechanism for satisfying INV 11; adopters MAY substitute semantic routing, dynamic composition, or RAG-style retrieval, provided the outcome holds (minimum-viable, no monolith, recorded in context_sources). The CARVE-OUT is meta_review and apply_meta — both legitimately read more breadth and are exempt from the INV 11 sub-check at Step 10 CONSUME.

Operationally: agents.config.yaml is now schema_version: 3 (was 2). Every agent block MUST declare family: alongside loads_bundle:. Step 4 DISPATCH ledger rows MUST emit context_sources (file-path list) AND context_selection_mechanism (one of: bundle, semantic-routing, dynamic-composition, other). Step 10 CONSUME rows MUST emit inv11_verdict (PASS|FAIL): FAIL when context_sources contains SYSTEM-BLUEPRINT.md, is empty, or context_selection_mechanism is missing (CARVE-OUT applies). inv11_verdict feeds into final_verdict alongside auth/schema/verification — a FAIL there blocks consume.

Worker write authority is per-role, NOT a blanket "workers DO write all their artifacts": executor.research / executor.commercial / wiki_ingest / kb_linter write their rich artifacts directly during dispatch (Step 4-5); planner / truthsayer / evaluator / pre_check / wiki_query return last_message and the orchestrator writes at Step 10 CONSUME after AUTH/SCHEMA/VERIFY pass. The §25 dispatch shim Step 10 enforces this; bypassing with a direct worker write defeats the gate.

If you are operating the codex-bridge adapter: the "Bridge binary missing" failure does NOT mean "the pipeline does not stop". It triggers a three-step degradation chain (try equivalent path → reroute inline → escalate and halt if no inline fallback) per 40-runtime/bootstrap-and-degradation.md:73-90 and adoption-guides/codex-bridge-adapter.md §5.

COPY END
```

## Section 3 — Validating the backport

Run, in order:

1. `tools/verify-frontmatter.sh --strict` — confirms `agents.config.yaml` advertises `schema_version: 3` and the new `family:` field is present on every agent.
2. `tools/verify-cross-refs.sh` — confirms the new INV 11 references in `INDEX.md`, `bundles/_README.md`, the dispatch-shim files, and the schema doc all resolve.
3. `tools/build-bundle.sh --check` — confirms bundle referential integrity (unaffected by INV 11 reframing; the check is bundle hygiene, not INV 11 enforcement).
4. **Smoke test — INV 11 enforcement positive path**: dispatch any non-meta_review role and confirm the DISPATCH row in `pipeline/verification-ledger.jsonl` carries non-empty `context_sources` + a named `context_selection_mechanism`, and the matching CONSUME row carries `inv11_verdict: PASS`.
5. **Smoke test — INV 11 enforcement negative path**: deliberately inject `SYSTEM-BLUEPRINT.md` into the `context_sources` of a test dispatch (do NOT do this on production state). Expected: Step 10 CONSUME emits `inv11_verdict: FAIL`, `final_verdict` is FAIL, the consume is rejected per `validation.on_validation_failure`. If the consume passes, your enforcement is incomplete.
6. **Smoke test — worker write authority**: dispatch `truthsayer` (read-only / advisory) and confirm the orchestrator writes `audit-report.md` at Step 10 CONSUME — not the worker mid-dispatch. If the worker writes directly, your INV 9 SCOPE wiring still has the pre-fix blanket reading.

## Section 4 — Why a backport directive instead of "just re-vendor"

Adopters who have layered local edits onto `commands/_delegate.md`, `agents.config.yaml`, or the adoption-guides copies cannot blindly overwrite their tree from a fresh `b1dea47..18f6b54` diff — they would lose the local edits. This guide enumerates the *minimum* surface that MUST move forward to remain INV-11-compliant; everything else is yours to keep.

If you have NOT layered local edits, the simpler path is a clean re-vendor at `18f6b54` or later, then re-run the validation in Section 3 against your own dispatches.
